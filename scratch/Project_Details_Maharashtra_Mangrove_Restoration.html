Project Description

The Maharashtra Mangrove Restoration project aims to restore degraded coastal mudflats through the active plantation and natural regeneration of mangrove species, primarily Avicennia marina. This project enhances local biodiversity, provides coastal protection against storm surges, and sequesters significant amounts of blue carbon. The initiative spans 12 distinct plantation sites and involves extensive local community participation in monitoring and maintenance.
